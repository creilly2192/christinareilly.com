Christina Reilly — Design System

A running-log aesthetic: playful, hand-touched, changelog-dated. Personal site, not a product UI.

Color

One brand accent, used consistently for links, badges, and highlights. The other three hues are decorative only — a fixed rotation for cards and book covers, never used as UI/brand color.

Typography

A running log of what I'm reading, writing, and thinking about.

Two typefaces only. No monospace — changelog-style tags use Instrument Sans, uppercase, wide letter-spacing, to read as "log" metadata without a third font.

Voice & micro-copy pattern

Every section — Working On, Currently Reading, Now Listening, Writing — uses the same dated, changelog-style status tag so a visitor can tell they're entries in one ledger by typography alone.

Section Tag format Example
Working On last touched [date] / since [date] LAST TOUCHED AUG 04 · SINCE MAY 2025
Currently Reading checked out / returned [date] CHECKED OUT APR 28
Now Listening on repeat since [date] ON REPEAT SINCE JUL 20
Writing (empty) [date] — status line AUG 04, 2026 — NOTHING PUBLISHED YET

Components & motifs

Folder-style section badges

＋▤✎

Rounded-square icon badges mark every section heading, centered glyph, wiggle on hover.

Bento-grid "Working On"

Cards are sized by real-life weight, not uniform tiles: the biggest, most personal item gets the largest tile; the day job is a wide banner; side projects are small. Each card tilts slightly and lifts on hover.

Retro browser-window hero

The hero headline sits inside a bordered browser-chrome card — colored title bar with three dot "traffic lights," hard-offset drop shadow (no blur), thick ink-colored borders. Reads as a nod to being a web developer without literal terminal syntax.

Playful hover states

  • "web" in the hero bounces letter-by-letter, each landing in a different card color
  • Working On cards tilt and lift
  • Section badges wiggle
  • The vinyl record can be "scratched" with a click
  • Book covers tilt on row hover

Layout rhythm

  • Generous whitespace between sections; each major section gets its own full-width band
  • Working On sits on a tinted band to separate it from the hero
  • Content max width keeps line lengths readable; hero type is large (~100px) and bold
  • Dark mode is a straight token swap — same layout, same components, inverted palette plus pure-black borders on the browser-window chrome (softer neutrals read muddy on dark)
